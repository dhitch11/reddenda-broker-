TITLE: Sacramento's Prices Are Public. Nobody Reads Them.
SUBTITLE: Every hospital rate in your market, to the cent, with the refusals showing.

- Line 1: land hard on "Sacramento" and "cent." Flat, factual, unhurried. This is the eight second hook, so no salesman lift at the end of the sentence.
- "Almost no human being has ever read it" - press "no human being." Then a full beat of silence before "Nobody hid it."
- "We got through the door." - drop the volume, do not raise it. Quiet confidence. Half second of air on either side.
- The site of care ladder: read the three dollar figures at the same steady pace, no escalation in the voice, and let the numbers escalate on their own. Only "which building" gets weight.
- "Are we paying too much." - say it as the employer would, plain and a little tired, not as a question you are answering. Then slow right down on "was a feeling."
- "Not insurers. Opacity." - two separate flat statements, clear gap between them. No edge, no accusation anywhere in this paragraph. Warmth on "pays for the room you are standing in."
- "Modelled, not measured" - the most deliberate words in the script. Slight slowdown, full articulation on both words. Same care on "refused" and "refusal" every time they appear.
- Closing: lift slightly on "a renewal you keep for five years," then settle. "Book a discovery call. Twenty minutes." is warm and matter of fact, not urgent. Final line, "holding the file," lands soft and stops. No music swell over it.
