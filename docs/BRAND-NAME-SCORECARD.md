# 1. THE BRIEF, AND THE ONE FINDING THAT CHANGED IT

The build was scoped 2026-08-06 as a product for **health insurance brokers, General Agents and
self-funded employers**. David ruled at 20:49 UTC: *"Fine, I'll create a separate full domain. Don't worry
about the name for now. Get everything built out."*

Research into where the money and the fear actually sit on the buy side turned up something that reframes
the naming brief:

**Plaintiffs have started naming brokers and consultants as co-defendants in ERISA fiduciary suits, not
just plan sponsors.** Willis Towers Watson, Mercer and Lockton are all named. In March 2026,
*Stern v. JPMorgan Chase* (S.D.N.Y.) became the first of these to survive a motion to dismiss on the
merits. Lewandowski (J&J) and Navarro (Wells Fargo) were dismissed on speculative injury, but the
direction is one way.

**Consequence for the name:** this is not "Reddenda for brokers." It is **the evidence layer for benefits
fiduciaries**, and the broker is one of three buyers. So:

> **Name it for the evidence, not for the channel.**

A name containing "broker" is wrong for two of the three buyers on day one. The GA sells *to* brokers.
The self-funded CFO is not one.

## 3.1 Detail

### 1. Praestanda.com — 92
*praestō*: to vouch for, to guarantee, to furnish, to make good. The gerundive *praestanda* means "the
things that must be vouched for," which is precisely the fiduciary artifact this product produces.

**Reddenda** and **Tuenda** are both already Latin gerundives in this estate. Adding a third completes a
visible naming architecture: **Reddenda** (provider side) · **Praestanda** (buy side) · **Tuenda** (the
intelligence layer both run on). Almost no healthtech company has a coherent naming system. It converts
"we launched a second website" into "we are a rate infrastructure house with market-facing surfaces,"
which is the difference between a feature story and a platform story in a VC room.

Zero trademark registrations worldwide, so it is the strongest and most defensible mark on this list.
The `-anda` ending keeps it distinct from Reddenda rather than rhyming with it.

**Cost:** ten letters, and David spells it on stage. That is the entire case against it.

### 2. Censenda.com — 90
*censeō*: to assess, to appraise, to rate, to value. The verb behind *census*.

Two things collide well here. It is the most precise meaning match available, and **"census" is the
single most-used word in group benefits.** Every group quote starts with the employer census. A broker
hears "Censenda" and lands on the right association instantly, which also mostly solves the spelling
problem. Zero marks worldwide.

**Cost:** `-enda` rhymes with Reddenda closely enough that the two blur when said back to back.

### 3. ProofBasis.com — 87
Two common English words. Zero spelling risk on a microphone, which is the highest-weighted criterion
for this launch. "On what basis did you decide" is the literal fiduciary question. Zero marks worldwide.
`.com`, `.io` and `.ai` are all free. Ceiling is perfect: broker, GA, TPA and CFO all buy it identically.

**Cost:** less distinctive than the Latin options, and a weaker mark because it is closer to suggestive
than arbitrary. Reads as competent B2B rather than category-defining.

### 4. SpreadBasis.com — 79
Maximum native fit. "Spread" is the loudest thing in the corpus (5.1x on a brain MRI in California
across 313,579 observations) and a word that room uses daily. Zero marks.

**Cost:** in insurance, "spread" also means commission spread, and CAA §202 compensation disclosure made
that a sensitive word with exactly this audience.

### 5. Aestimanda.com — 76
*aestimō*: to appraise. Purest meaning match of every option here, since appraisal is literally the
product. Same house system as Praestanda.

**Cost:** the `ae-` opening is a genuine spelling failure in American English. One unrelated Italian wine
mark exists (classes 33/35/39, no conflict). Best meaning on the list, worst phonetics of the Latin set.

### 6. KnownRate.com — 75
The whole promise in plain English: the rate is known, not estimated. Aligned to HARD RULE 1. Spells
itself. Zero marks.

**Cost:** close to descriptive, which means a weaker registration and a name competitors can crowd.

### 7. CensusBasis.com — 72
Genuine double meaning: the broker's census document, and the Roman assessment of value. Native
vocabulary, zero marks.

**Cost:** collides mentally with the Census Bureau and with Census, the data company.

### 8. Emendanda.com — 68
*emendō*: to correct. "The things to be corrected" is an accurate description of an overpriced plan.
Zero marks, clean spelling, real house fit.

**Cost:** "emend" is obscure to nearly everyone, and the meaning reads faintly accusatory toward the
buyer being sold to.

### 9. broker.reddenda.com — 46 as canonical, 94 as launch alias
**As the permanent brand it is wrong.** It hard-codes the narrowest of three buyers into the identity. It
accrues no separable equity, so it can never be spun out, sold or funded on its own. And it sits one DNS
label from the provider-side product, which is the one artifact that makes the adjacency legible to a
customer.

**As a 20-day launch alias it is excellent and should be used.** Free, instant, inherits reddenda.com's
existing authority and sending reputation, and catches everything said on stage. Use it for 20 days,
301 it forever after.

### 10. reddenda.broker — 28
Verified available against the authoritative Identity Digital registry, so the option is real. Still the
weakest here.

- You rent the category word from the registry rather than owning it.
- Every ceiling problem of #9, with none of the parent domain's authority or sending reputation.
- Unfamiliar TLDs get filtered by corporate networks. **This is measured on this estate, not
  theoretical:** `futureful.app` is currently "Not Rated" in FortiGuard and ICP networks are serving
  prospects a block page today. Brokers sit behind exactly that class of firewall.
- Spoken aloud, "reddenda dot broker" sends most of the room to `reddendabroker.com`.

**Buy it for defense at registration cost. Do not brand on it.**

# 4. VERIFICATION METHOD

Four independent layers. Stated so it can be re-run rather than trusted.

1. **Registry RDAP.** Verisign for `.com`, Identity Digital for `.broker`. HTTP 404 is authoritative
   unregistered. **This is not a reseller lookup**, which can show false positives on held names.
2. **DNS delegation.** Every name below returns an empty NS record. No parked page, no dormant owner,
   no expiring-soon squatter.
3. **Trademark.** TMview, the EUIPO-run aggregator covering USPTO, WIPO and national offices.
   **The endpoint was validated against a control before any zero was trusted: `aetna` returns 625 marks.**
   An unvalidated zero from a trademark API is a false-negative machine.
4. **Operating-company sweep** for commercial use without a filing. Zero collisions across all names.

# 6. DOMAIN ARCHITECTURE

```
<brand>.com            canonical marketing host
app.<brand>.com        the tools

broker.reddenda.com    301 -> <brand>.com    launch alias; inherits reddenda.com authority
reddenda.broker        301 -> <brand>.com    defensive, verified free
reddendabroker.com     301 -> <brand>.com    defensive; this is the string people actually type
reddendahealth.com     301 -> reddenda.com   defensive, unrelated to this build
```

**Do not use `app.reddenda.com/broker`.** That path permanently ties buy-side URLs to the provider brand
and is the one link a provider customer would screenshot.

Endorse rather than co-brand: a single footer line, "Built on the Reddenda rate platform." That earns the
parent's credibility and the platform narrative without putting both sides of the table on one page.

**HARD RULE 13 note:** rule 13 says all tools live on `app.reddenda.com`. A new brand's own app host is a
documented exception David created on 2026-08-06. This should stay recorded so a later lane does not file
it as a violation, which has happened before on this estate.

# 7. THE CRITICAL PATH

**A new sending domain needs roughly two to three weeks of reputation warm-up before cold outreach lands
in inboxes.**

- The **site** can launch on `broker.reddenda.com` at any time, because reddenda.com already has sending
  reputation.
- The **outreach** cannot. The follow-up email to everyone met on Aug 26 is the point of the event.

**Register on Aug 20 and the follow-up goes to spam.** These are two different deadlines and only one of
them moves. The domain decision is on the critical path for outreach, not just for the site.

Aug 26 is 20 days from this document.
