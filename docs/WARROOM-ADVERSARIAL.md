# WAR ROOM: ADVERSARIAL VERDICTS (10 refuters)

## [SERIOUS sev5] Liability and third-party reliance: who can sue Reddenda over one of these artifacts, and whether anything in the contract stack stops them

**Finding:** The plan's stated design axis is that the artifact leaves the login and is relied on by someone who never contracted with Reddenda. It says so in writing, repeatedly: "gets forwarded to the CFO without the advisor present" (tool 1), "the recipient needs no login, no account and no link that expires mid-meeting" (tool 3), "the downstream broker's own logo and license... no account for anyone downstream" (tool 5). Reddenda's entire liability posture is contractual and reaches none of those people. I read the live pages: /Users/user/reimburseos-v3-build/terms.html section 01 defines the service as "a reimbursement-intelligence platform for independent medical practices," and the strings broker, General Agent, self-funded, plan sponsor and fiduciary appear zero times in the file. Section 07 caps aggregate liability at the greater of $100 or twelve months of fees. Section 08 indemnity runs only to Reddenda for the user's own misuse, so it does nothing when the user's client sues the user over our number. Section 11 picks California law and Placer County. Every one of those provisions binds the signing user only. The CFO, the plan sponsor, the trustee and the losing RFP bidder are non-signatories, so the cap does not attach to them, the forum clause does not attach to them, and the "not advice" section (which is itself scoped to "The free Snapshot") does not attach to them. That is Restatement (Second) of Torts section 552 in textbook form, and under the California law the terms themselves select, Bily v. Arthur Andersen (1992) 3 Cal.4th 370 is the controlling frame: no duty to all foreseeable users, but real exposure to the limited group the supplier intended to influence in a specific transaction. The plan documents that intent in its own persona list. Posture to be confirmed with counsel, but the fact pattern is ours by design, not by accident.

Four things in the plan make it worse than the generic version.

(a) Tool 5 removes Reddenda from the header and prints a downstream producer's license number on the face of 200 machine-generated documents with no human review step, delivered by webhook into a GA quote engine. That strips the relying party's ability to identify or evaluate the source while leaving us identifiable in the footer for a plaintiff, and it designs out the intervening professional judgment that would otherwise put the duty on the broker. It is also the standard E&O denial fight: the carrier declines because the document was not the producer's professional act, then subrogates against us. The plan concedes the mechanism ("mails it out under someone else's insurance license") and mitigates it only with fail-closed cells.

(b) The plan's only defense against reliance is a banned-string copy gate. Strings regulate wording. Reliance attaches to function. A document titled "Market Evidence Record," structured as numbered exhibits plus a decision record with attendees, alternatives considered, rationale and next review date, retained for exactly the ERISA section 413 six-year limitations period, functions as a fiduciary process artifact no matter which words it avoids. That same six-year byte-reproducible archive also makes Reddenda the best discovery target in every case involving a customer: our suppression telemetry proves what the fiduciary was shown and when, which is worth more to a plaintiff than to the defendant we sold it to, and we carry the custodian cost with no revenue attached.

(c) The single number most likely to be relied on is not supportable. Tool 2 is P0 and renders a "modeled PLAN-LIABILITY impact, range" from a Medicare fee-schedule setting differential (HOPD/ASC 1.85x to 2.03x) multiplied by user-supplied units. The plan's own cut list forbids exactly this and calls it "the single claim most likely to end the company," then ships it. The differential is Medicare's, not the plan's, and the commercial site-of-service variance was destroyed at write time by uq_pcr_coalesce and is provably unrecoverable. The upper bound of that range describes a payment relationship the employer is not party to. "The user supplied the units" is not a defense when we supplied the multiplicand and the arithmetic.

(d) ERISA preemption does not backstop it. Governmental plans (ERISA 4(b)(1)) and church plans are exempt outright, and the corpus's own employer tail is full of them: CalPERS, State of New York, Metropolitan Government of Nashville, School Board of Orange County FL. Public entities buy by RFP, which tool 7 is built to arm. For those buyers there is no section 514 shield over a state-law misrepresentation claim, and tool 7's RFP mode adds a plaintiff class nobody accounted for: a losing bidder scored against a Medicare basis we computed ourselves from RVU x GPCI x CF rather than a CMS locality-exact file, in a market where 93 of 925 state-plus-zip3 pairs are locality-ambiguous. That bidder has no contract with us, a documented economic loss, and counsel.

**What breaks:** A 200-life self-funded employer's CFO receives the Site-of-Service Exhibit from her broker in the finalist room. It carries the broker's logo and license number, no Reddenda header, and a modeled annual impact range whose top line is roughly $180K on ASC steerage for ortho and GI, built from a Medicare HOPD/ASC ratio times unit counts the broker typed. She amends the SPD, adds a site-of-care requirement, and takes the change to the board. Twelve months later the plan's actual spend is flat, because the network's commercial contract prices the ASC near the HOPD for those codes and nothing in our corpus could ever have seen that. She sues the broker. The broker's E&O carrier denies on the ground that an auto-generated vendor document he never reviewed is not his professional act, and subrogates against TwinFlame. The CFO adds Reddenda directly, and she is not bound by terms.html: not by the $100 cap, not by the Placer County forum, not by section 02, because she never accepted any of it and the service definition in section 01 does not even describe her. Discovery produces this planning document, in which Reddenda wrote that the artifact is designed to reach the CFO without the advisor present, and its own cut list calling the exact number on the page the claim most likely to end the company. If the buyer is a school district or a state plan, ERISA preemption is not available at all, and a parallel bid protest names our Medicare basis as the defective scoring term. The company-level damages are not the judgment. They are that the first liability event is co-branded, so it lands on every GA relationship simultaneously, and a GA panel already said the carrier relationship is worth more to them than the tool.

**Fix:** Five changes, all before the first broker artifact ships. None of them is a wording change.

1. Bind the reader, not just the buyer. Make the expiring view link the universal delivery path for every artifact, including PDFs. First open presents a one-screen Recipient Notice with scope-of-use and non-reliance terms and an explicit accept that records recipient, timestamp and artifact version hash. Any raw file carries the same notice as page one, plus a live verification URL and a "void if superseded" line. This does not manufacture privity, but it is the largest single factor on justifiable reliance and on whether the supplier defined and limited the class it intended to influence.

2. Restore the intervening professional judgment, and never print a license number without it. Batch produces drafts only. Publication requires a per-artifact affirmative act by the named producer on a review screen showing suppression count, failed cells and vintage, with "I have reviewed and adopt this exhibit" recorded with identity and time. The GA's real ask is generation volume, not unreviewed mailing volume, so throughput survives. Tool 5 as specified, which auto-mails 200 documents under a third party's license with no review step, must not be built.

3. Delete the modeled dollar from tools 2 and 3 in v1. Render the Medicare-reference setting differential as a ratio only, labeled as a Medicare-reference ratio, and show the user's unit count as a unit count. No multiplication, no range, no plan-liability line, no member-share range. It is one line of arithmetic and it is the line that creates the damages number; the ratio alone carries the entire steerage argument. A dollar figure returns only behind a separately accepted engagement where the customer supplies its own commercial rates.

4. Restructure tool 4 into a query receipt and get the archive off our infrastructure. Keep what was asked, what was returned, what was suppressed and why, at what vintage. Delete the blank decision-record template entirely: supplying the deliberation form is what makes us the process advisor, and the customer's own counsel supplies it. Issue the receipt to the customer, retain only a hash for reproducibility, and cap telemetry retention at 90 days. Do not hold six years of customers' fiduciary deliberations on Reddenda systems.

5. Rewrite the contract stack first. terms.html section 01 scopes the service to independent medical practices, so the section 07 cap may not even attach to broker use today. Ship a broker/GA master agreement carrying: an express no-third-party-beneficiary clause, a flow-down the broker must present to every recipient, an indemnity running from the broker to Reddenda for claims by his clients arising from exhibits he adopted, and a requirement that he carry E&O covering this activity. Add a Reddenda-side liability cap keyed to fees actually paid, and either give tool 7's RFP mode an explicit non-reliance statement addressed to bidders plus issuer adoption of the Medicare basis at its own election, or do not ship RFP mode.

## [FATAL sev5] TiC data forensics: estimator validity of the contract-grain recompute

**Finding:** The plan's load-bearing fix, the "contract-grain" recompute that gates all of P0, is uncomputable as specified, cites a regulation it cannot satisfy, and is validated on the single cell where the wrong fix accidentally looks right. Six measured defects.

(1) IT CANNOT BE BUILT ON THE NAMED TABLE. npi_payer_cpt_rate columns are exactly: payer, npi, cpt, state, negotiated_rate, rate_observed_min, rate_observed_p90, rate_observed_max, observed_n, source_file, ingested_at. The spec's key DISTINCT (payer, source_file, billing_class, modifier, negotiated_rate) names two columns that do not exist there. It degrades silently to DISTINCT (payer, source_file, rate), which blends PC/TC/global, the exact defect (b) the plan says must close first.

(2) THE 45 CFR 149.140(b)(1) CITATION IS FALSE. That rule needs a contract key. Measured on 136,487 sampled rows of payer_contracted_rates: tin 0 populated, payer_plan 0 populated, negotiation_arrangement 0 populated. No contract key, no plan key, no ffs/bundle/capitation flag exists. The key counts distinct PRICE POINTS per payer, not contracts.

(3) IT DOUBLE-COUNTS FILE VINTAGES. source_file is a file, not a contract, and the corpus holds several vintages per payer. bcbstx carries both lake_bcbstx_2026_07_16 and lake_bcbstx_v2_2026_07_28. A rate present in both counts as two "contracts."

(4) IT IS 38 TO 58 PERCENT GHOST RATES BY CONSTRUCTION. Share of the deduped distribution made of rates appearing exactly once in the state: CA 99214 38.4%, CA 70553 41.4%, CA 45378 42.4%, FL 45378 43.2%, FL 99214 45.9%, FL 70553 57.6%. Those same rates are 0.7% to 14.5% of rows. Up to 55x weight amplification of the maximum-likelihood ghost population (Health Affairs Scholar 2025: 95.7% ghost rate at individual-NPI level).

(5) THE MECHANISM, MEASURED. TX 99214: the 8 most widely applied rates cover 11,210 of 30,128 rows (37% of the market) and become 8 of 4,014 observations (0.2% of the answer). The 969 singleton rates are 3.2% of rows and 24% of the answer, and their median is $134.27 against $116.18 for the 500-plus band. That gap is the entire lift.

(6) IT MOVES CLEAN CELLS VIOLENTLY AND IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. TX 99214 has 72 junk rows of 30,128 (0.24%): p50 $115.00 to $131.10 (+14%), p90 $186.60 to $239.22 (+28%). CA 70553 has zero sub-$2 rows: p50 $1,448.13 to $848.31 (-41%). The direction is set by whether a cell's standardized rates sit above or below its scattered ones, which is unknowable in advance.

Contract dedup does not cure the NY $1 p25. It masks it, dropping junk from 29.49% of rows to 1.88% of observations while leaving 58 junk values in the served distribution. It looks like a cure only because that cell's junk (8,365 rows at exactly $0.95) is more replicated than its legitimate rates. That is a property of one cell, not a fix.

**What breaks:** The gate goes green for the wrong reason, which is how this ships. The team builds the recompute, tests it on NY 99214 because that is the cell the plan names, sees p25 move $0.95 to $106.82 and p50 to $140.40, matches the plan's predicted $106.93/$141.27, declares the contamination defect closed, and unblocks Metro Rate Exhibit as P0. Every downstream tool reads that engine.

Then: Tool 7's RFP attachment prints the P90 and requires every bidder to respond as a percent of it, setting the bidding unit for a plan sponsor's live procurement. On TX 99214 that P90 is $239.22 where the row-weighted figure is $186.60, a 28% overstatement on a code with 0.24% contamination and no imaging or site-of-service excuse. A carrier reconciles against its own book, cannot reproduce the number by any method, and the exhibit becomes the argument instead of ending it.

Tool 5 mails the same cells as 200 co-branded PDFs under a downstream broker's own insurance license and producer number. Tool 4 files them as dated, immutable, six-year-retained fiduciary evidence whose methodology appendix cites 45 CFR 149.140(b)(1) as authority, a citation that is false because tin, payer_plan and negotiation_arrangement are all 0% populated and no contract can be counted once when no contract is identified at all. A benefits attorney checks the cite on the first call.

The n_contracts figure printed on every cell to establish credibility is itself fabricated: for TX 99214 the four candidate methods print 30,128, 4,014, 3,047 and 2,281 for the same market, a 13x swing.

**Fix:** Do not build the recompute as written. Five changes, then revalidate.

1. DELETE the 45 CFR 149.140(b)(1) citation from the spec, the methodology appendix, the PDF footer and all copy. Never print "n_contracts". Contract grain is uncomputable while tin is 0% populated. Label the unit honestly: "distinct filed price points per payer, deduplicated across providers, not weighted by volume and not weighted by contract."

2. SOURCE THE REBUILD FROM payer_contracted_rates, not npi_payer_cpt_rate. Only the former carries billing_class, modifier, negotiated_type and service_code. The plan's stated fix cannot run on the table it names, and this is a materially larger build than the plan scopes.

3. FILTER BEFORE REWEIGHTING, in this order: drop lower(negotiated_type) in (percentage, derived, per diem); drop 0 < rate <= 2.00 and rate < 0.25x the Medicare locality non-facility rate for that code; keep only the newest source_file per payer; drop expired rows; split billing_class and modifier into separate labelled series rather than blending them. Fan-out reduction comes last, never first.

4. REPLACE VALUE DEDUP WITH PAYER-EQUALIZED WEIGHTING, then drop rates appearing on a single NPI in the cell. Measured on TX 99214, payer-equalized gives p50 $130.59 and p90 $236.87 against dedup's $131.10 and $239.22, so it corrects fan-out just as well without handing 38 to 58 percent of the distribution to single-NPI ghosts. Gate and print distinct PAYERS (minimum 5) and distinct NPIs.

5. ADD AN ESTIMATOR-DIVERGENCE GATE as an npm run gates check. Compute p50 and p90 by all four methods on every publishable cell and suppress the cell to "insufficient reliable public data" where the P90 range across methods exceeds 15% of the median method. TX 99214 spans 28% today and would correctly suppress.

REVALIDATE ON TX 99214 AND CA 70553, NEVER ON NY 99214. NY 99214 is the one cell in the corpus where the wrong estimator produces a right-looking answer, and the plan selected exactly that cell as its proof. Until the divergence gate is green, Metro Rate Exhibit stays behind Site-of-Service Exhibit, and Channel Batch and Market Evidence Record do not ship at all.

## [FATAL sev5] Execution and delivery realism (scope, sequencing, refresh cost, support burden, month-three failure)

**Finding:** The P0 that gates 6 of 8 tools cannot be built against any table in this estate as written, and the team has ALREADY shipped past it with a gate that is structurally anti-correlated with the defect it is meant to catch. This is not a month-three risk. It is live now.

1. THE SPEC DOES NOT COMPILE. The plan's prerequisite is "percentiles over DISTINCT (payer, source_file, billing_class, modifier, negotiated_rate) from npi_payer_cpt_rate". Measured live: npi_payer_cpt_rate has exactly 11 columns (payer, npi, cpt, state, negotiated_rate, rate_observed_min/p90/max, observed_n, source_file, ingested_at). No billing_class. No modifier. The regulatory citation the whole product leans on does not survive its own implementation.

This is a monthly job, forever, on a 770 GB / 511-table database that also serves the provider product.

3. WHAT SHIPPED INSTEAD IS WORSE THAN NOTHING. src/lib/honesty.ts judge() gates on DOLLAR_FLOOR=$5, monotonicity, p75/p25<=25, and n>=100/500. Run CA 70553 through it, measured live today: row grain n=10,561, p25 $1,070.30, p50 $1,448.13, p75 $1,998.12. Every check passes (p75/p25 = 1.87), n=10,561 >= N_CONFIDENT, so confidenceLabel returns "10,561 filings" at HIGH confidence. Contract grain on the same cell: n=710, p50 $848.31. The shipped surface serves a median 70.7% too high, on a denominator inflated 14.9x, calls fan-out rows "filings", and stamps it high confidence. The gate cannot see this by construction: replicating one payer's schedule tightens the distribution and raises n, so MORE contamination produces a HIGHER confidence badge. The gate is anti-correlated with the defect. The lane's own docs/HANDOFF-DATA.md fix list (partition negotiated_type, preserve billing_class, add a floor, rebuild) does not mention fan-out at all, so the data lane will do the requested work, the $1 cells will vanish, the gate will go green, and $1,448 will still be served.

The plan requires POST /api/broker/exhibit/render returning a PDF URL, a 200-PDF zip with expiring links and a webhook, a DOCX, and a byte-identical-for-six-years immutable PDF. ## [FATAL sev5] Domain and brand architecture: broker.reddenda.com vs a section of app.reddenda.com vs a distinct brand

**Finding:** The domain question is unanswerable as posed, because all three options ship an artifact that Reddenda's own published charter forbids. Measured live 2026-08-06: https://reddenda.com/covenant returns 200, 77,366 bytes, carries `<meta name="robots" content="index, follow">`, is titled "The Covenant. The rate index that will never take a dollar from a health plan", and is linked 3x from index.html via href="/covenant". Its Covenant II draft charter language, served to any anonymous request right now, reads: "No score, report, artifact, data feed, application programming interface, or export produced by the Corporation shall be sold, licensed, furnished, or otherwise made available to any person for the purpose of (i) reducing the amount paid to a health care provider." The page then names what that clause exists to kill: "the FCRA exposure, the buy-side acquirer conflict, and the plan-sponsor conflict, in one clause." The proposed buyer IS the plan sponsor. Seven of the eight proposed tools are inside the prohibition by their own spec: tool 2 ships "a draft SPD amendment line" for site-of-care steerage, tool 7 is "an RFP attachment forcing every bidder to answer in percent of the Medicare locality rate", and the plan's own rationale says the artifact "becomes a BOR letter". Those are procurement instruments whose purpose is a lower unit price. Reddenda would be arguing purpose against its own product copy. Covenant I compounds it: a Prohibited Party is any entity above 10% trailing-four-quarter revenue from "third-party administration, administrative-services-only contracts, pharmacy benefit management, or out-of-network repricing", which screens a meaningful share of the GA and national-brokerage FIRM tier. Covenant III forbids computing or transmitting "a target price, a recommended offer or ask", which the tool 7 RFP basis does for a buyer, and furnishing one metro price reference to competing plan sponsors in one CBSA is the buy-side mirror of the Sherman Act information-exchange theory the homepage itself cites against a repricer. Four separate disproofs of the "pick a host and move on" framing. (1) SEO is a non-argument and cannot break the tie: reddenda-app/src/app/layout.tsx:63 sets robots {index:false, follow:false} as the root default, src/app/robots.ts hardcodes host and sitemap to app.reddenda.com, src/app/sitemap.ts hardcodes BASE to app.reddenda.com, layout.tsx:55 hardcodes metadataBase to app.reddenda.com, and next.config.ts defines redirects() with no rewrites() at all. A broker.reddenda.com alias on the same Netlify site would serve a robots.txt declaring another host as canonical, a sitemap pointing at another host, and og/canonical URLs resolved through another host's metadataBase. It would rank nothing. The estate's only indexable surface is reddenda.com, which is the provider covenant site, so buy-side SEO under this brand means putting broker acquisition copy on the same indexed host as "will never take a dollar from a health plan". (2) A subdomain cannot be launched quietly. The live apex certificate (Let's Encrypt, read via TLS today) carries explicit SANs: *.reimburseos.com, partner.reddenda.com, reddenda.com, reimburseos.com, www.reddenda.com. Explicit per-host SANs, no wildcard for reddenda.com. partner.reddenda.com is the precedent sitting in that list right now. broker.reddenda.com is published to public Certificate Transparency logs at issuance, permanently, queryable by every competitive-intel tool. ## [SERIOUS sev4] Positioning honesty: is "dispersion, not level" a real finding or a rationalization for a corpus that cannot support level claims

**Finding:** The thesis is honest about the world and dishonest about the value, and in THIS corpus it is empirically inverted. Four things, in descending order of how badly they hurt.

1. THE SPREAD IS SUBSTANTIALLY MANUFACTURED BY OUR OWN DEFECTS, AND EVERY DEFECT PUSHES THE SAME WAY. Contract fan-out, decimal multipliers filed in a dollar column, unlabelled PC/TC/global blending (26.08% of rows, up to 3.165x), unlabelled site-of-service blending (>=1.5x on 22.6% of rows) and BlueCard spillover all WIDEN measured dispersion. Not one known defect narrows it. The plan's own measurement is the proof: NY 99214 at row grain is p25 $0.95 / p75 $234.26, an interquartile ratio of 246x; the identical cell deduplicated to contracts per 45 CFR 149.140(b)(1) is $106.93 / $195.70, a ratio of 1.83x. One methodological correction removed 99.3% of the measured spread and nothing about the market changed. The pattern holds across codes: 99214 TX, office-only with no facility variant and no PC/TC, spreads 1.60x ($93/$115/$149); 70553 CA, which the ground truth explicitly says is a blend of global, PC-only and TC-only across office and hospital outpatient, spreads 5.07x ($258/$589/$1,309). The dispersion signal tracks the presence of unlabelled mixing, not market structure. Independently corroborating: peer_rate_dist shows modal_pct >= 90 on 801,446 of 1,194,087 cells (67.1%), meaning that where a payer/metro/code cell is clean enough to have a dominant contracted value, there is almost no dispersion in it at all.

2. THE FUNNEL IS BUILT ON THAT ARTIFACT BY NAME. HANDOFF-MARKETING.md step 2 of the acquisition funnel is literally "THE FLINCH: they see their own market and the spread is worse than they believed," and the hero cinematic is specified as "hundreds of actual rate points settling into a distribution, the spread becoming visible." The emotional event that converts a cold visitor is a reaction to a number we know is inflated by our own write-time and rollup defects. That is not a copy problem a string gate can fix. Note also that the proposed broker suppression gate suppresses p75/p25 > 12, so the served dispersion is right-censored at 12x, invisibly; Book Scan then ranks a customer's entire book on that censored statistic, which is simultaneously our contamination signature. The groups that sort to the top of an account manager's Q4 list are disproportionately the metros where our data is dirtiest.

3. THE HEALTH ECONOMICS SAYS DISPERSION IS REAL AND NEARLY INERT WITHOUT A MECHANISM THE PLAN REFUSES TO SHIP. Cooper/Craig/Gaynor/Van Reenen (QJE 2019) supports that commercial spending variation is price-driven and that within-market price variation is large. It does not support that showing the variation moves money. The transparency-tool evidence is the opposite: Desai et al. (JAMA 2016) found offering employees a price transparency tool produced no reduction in outpatient spending at roughly 10% take-up; the lower-limb MRI work (Chernew, Cooper et al., NBER) found patients bypass cheaper providers because the REFERRING PHYSICIAN, not the price, determines site. What does work is reference pricing and site-of-care benefit design (the CalPERS work by Robinson, Brown and Whaley: roughly 20% on colonoscopy and double digits on labs, cataract and arthroscopy), because it changes the member's marginal price. Every one of those levers is either a settlor act or requires naming a facility, and the plan correctly cuts named-facility shortlists (tin 0% populated; individual-NPI p50 for 70553 CA equals the hospital-taxonomy p50 to the cent) and cuts RBP multiples. So the chain is: dispersion is monetized only through steerage, steerage requires naming a site or setting a floor, both are cut, therefore the artifact shows a variance the buyer cannot act on with the artifact. The plan bans the sentence "you can save $X" while the percentile grid makes that claim non-verbally. This is the estate's own recurring failure mode: a gate applied to the string while the inference has already been delivered. The sibling data lane reached the same verdict from the other direction: "it can render a spread but never N% of what, cheaper where, or whose price."

4. THE TWO-SIDED CONFLICT IS WRITTEN DOWN AND THEN NEVER DISCLOSED. HANDOFF-BRAND-DOMAIN.md:29 states it plainly: "This product tells employers where they are overpaying. Reddenda tells providers where they are underpaid. Same corpus, opposite side of the table." That was ruled skipped as a NAMING question. It is not the same question as DISCLOSURE, and there is no conflict disclosure anywhere in the plan: not in the Metro Rate Exhibit source footer, not in the Market Evidence Record methodology appendix, not on the RFP attachment that carries our methodology into a third party's procurement. The plan builds an identity firewall so a broker cannot read provider tools, and builds nothing to tell the broker those tools exist and are aimed at them. Reddenda's provider-side promise is causal: help the provider move toward local P90. In LA CBSA 31080 the provider product's target for 70553 is p90 $1,605 while the broker exhibit shows that same distribution as the employer's evidence. If both products work they cancel, and the buyer is never told.

**What breaks:** Two concrete failures, both in the room where the deal is decided.

FINALIST ROOM, Q4. The advisor puts the Metro Rate Exhibit on the table showing a 5.07x spread on imaging. The incumbent carrier rep, who has seen MRF analytics for three years, asks two questions: "is that professional, technical or global, and is it office or hospital outpatient." The honest answer printed on our own page is "all settings and components, unlabelled." The second question is "so what do you want us to do about it," and the product has no answer, because it cannot name a facility, cannot set a reference multiple, and cannot quantify a dollar. The advisor loses the room and the exhibit is what lost it. This is not hypothetical spread-guessing: the ground truth already attributes most of the imaging spread to that exact blend.

DEPOSITION OR COMPETITIVE DISPLACEMENT, 12 to 24 months out. The Market Evidence Record, sold to a named plan fiduciary who "buys risk documentation more readily than analytics," is a numbered exhibit in a plan decision file. Opposing counsel or a competing consultant establishes in one search that the vendor who produced the market evidence also sells a service to raise rates for providers in that market, and that no disclosure appears on the face of the document. The exhibit is void, the fiduciary is worse off than with no document, and the buyer is a CAA section 202 disclosure-regime professional who will treat an undisclosed vendor conflict as disqualifying across their whole book. One incident propagates through a GA channel that mails the same artifact under two hundred brokers' licenses.

Third, quieter failure: Book Scan misdirects an entire fourth quarter. It ranks 38 groups by a dispersion statistic that is our contamination fingerprint, censored at 12x by our own gate, with no utilization anywhere in the corpus. The account manager works the six groups where our data is dirtiest and skips the ones where it is clean.

**Fix:** Replace the thesis. Sell LEVEL against a public reference and SETTING, not spread. Five changes.

1. HEADLINE UNIT BECOMES PERCENT OF THE MEDICARE LOCALITY RATE, computed from medicare_pfs_rvu x medicare_gpci 2026 x CF, at metro grain, on the p50 only. "This metro's published in-network band for this basket sits at 176% to 186% of the 2026 Medicare locality rate" is a level claim, symmetric with the provider side, in the unit this audience already trades (RAND puts employer hospital payment in the low-to-mid 200s percent of Medicare; re-verify the exact round and figure before it enters copy). It is a central-tendency claim, which is the robust statistic, rather than a p25 claim, which is where every contamination mode lives. It also gives the spread the thing the data lane says it lacks: something to express it against. A flat cell (modal_pct >= 90) becomes a good finding at this unit instead of looking like broken data.

2. LEAD WITH SETTING, SCOPED HONESTLY. Site of care is the only lever with a credible savings literature behind it and the only clean data we hold. But size it correctly: rank on HOPD versus ASC, two real facility settings, and treat office as a third site only on the 1,415 codes where nonfac_rate <> fac_rate, because 5,517 of 7,647 CA codes (72.1%) have nonfac exactly equal to fac and ranking that spread produces "steer a distal ulna arthroplasty to an office" nonsense. Label it a Medicare-reference ratio every time. Tool 2 becomes the thesis, not the warm-up.

3. DEMOTE DISPERSION TO A GATED, LABELLED, SECONDARY FIGURE, and never a ranking input. If a spread renders, it renders only on contract-deduplicated cells, only with the exclusion counts and n_contracts printed, and with the censoring stated on the face ("cells above 12x interquartile ratio are suppressed as unreliable, so this figure is bounded"). Re-base Book Scan on percent-of-Medicare level plus basket ASC-eligibility share, both level facts. Re-cut the hero: the cinematic should resolve a cloud of points onto a Medicare reference line, not into a wide distribution. "THE FLINCH" is retired as a funnel stage.

4. PRINT THE CONFLICT ON EVERY ARTIFACT. One line in the non-removable source footer of every exhibit, every co-branded PDF, every Market Evidence Record and the RFP attachment: "Reddenda also sells rate benchmarking to healthcare providers, including providers in this market.
